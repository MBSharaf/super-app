## Phase 3 — Pitches & bookings (started 2026-10-17)

Decisions for this phase:
- Hours are Cairo local hours (Africa/Cairo); dates are `yyyy-MM-dd`. A booking hour belongs to the day it starts.
- **Overnight hours:** a pitch open until 2 AM keeps one row per day with `CloseHour` up to 30 (25 = 1 AM of the next night). This needs the CHECK on `pitch.OpeningHours` widened from `BETWEEN 1 AND 24` to `BETWEEN 1 AND 30` — a small migration the owner applies with `Update-Database`; the demo seed then stores the real closing hours.
- One booking per pitch, date and hour is guaranteed by the database (`booking.BookingSlots` unique index); holds last 10 minutes.
- Payments are Phase 5: until then a booking is confirmed through the **simulated gateway** (or "pay at the venue" where the pitch allows cash). The money tables are filled in Phase 5.
- Package QR: one signed token per package (and per single booking); scans are idempotent; reasons for a rejected scan follow API §3.6.
